Helpful details

Frequently asked questions

Read these basics before placing an order. Your individual licence document is the controlling agreement for the purchase.

When can I download my beat?

Only after the payment provider confirms the transaction and the store's verification function returns a paid status. Keep the checkout reference if you need support.

Why does a download link expire?

Secure download links are temporary by design. Download and save your files promptly. If a link expires, contact support with your payment reference for help.

Does Unlimited Commercial mean exclusive?

No, not automatically. The Unlimited Commercial Licence and Exclusive Rights are separate options. Read the signed licence for permitted use, restrictions, ownership and exclusivity.

What if I paid but don't see my files?

Do not immediately pay again. Keep your Paystack reference and email, then contact Prince Michael Mix so the order can be checked.

Can I request custom production?

Yes. Use the project enquiry form and include your genre, goals, deadline and any reference tracks.

Are all beats available in every format?

Available formats and licences depend on the options shown for each beat and the written terms. Contact us if you need a specific deliverable.
